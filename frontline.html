NATIONAL COMMAND

ECONOMY · PRODUCTION · GOVERNMENT · FOREIGN POLICY

ECONOMY & STRATEGIC MATERIALS

Resources are finite national stockpiles. Industrial production consumes steel, oil, coal, rubber and aluminum; extraction replenishes them gradually.

ARMAMENT PRODUCTION

Production uses a share of the national military budget and available factory capacity. Damaged or depleted equipment can be replaced over time.

NATIONAL MOBILIZATION

Mobilization increases recruitment and military expenditure but places pressure on national stability. Doctrine changes how forces perform when attacking or defending.

NATIONAL DECISIONS

Major decisions have treasury costs, duration, and stability or economic consequences. AI governments choose policies automatically unless a player has recently intervened.

FOREIGN MINISTRY

The target weighs the request against military strength, political stability and its diplomatic disposition. Rejection damages relations and can increase regional tension.

AI FOREIGN-POLICY PROFILE

AI personalities shape mobilization, industrial priorities, war posture and the likelihood of seeking alliances. Defensive alliances can form and dissolve as the diplomatic situation changes.

WEATHER & OPERATIONAL CONDITIONS

Seasonal snow, rain, storms and fog affect supply and combat efficiency. Use the map-layer selector to view weather across Europe.

AIR SUPERIORITY & FRONT INTELLIGENCE

Fighters, bombers, technology, weather and enemy air strength determine local air advantage. Air power contributes a bounded modifier to land operations.
