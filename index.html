SIMULATOR GUIDE

How to play

Tap a country or province to inspect it. Drag the map to pan and pinch to zoom. The simulation advances month by month while unpaused.

Real World: uses a geographic country map loaded from World Atlas. Country borders are real, while initial economic and demographic values are illustrative estimates.

Fictional World: uses an original procedural province map with invented nations and terrain.

Important: this is an early playable prototype. Combat, economy and AI are simplified game models, not real-world forecasts.
